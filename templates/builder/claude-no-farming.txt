# Principles (from community no-haul guides):
#   - Mines are your ONLY income. Rush wood/stone, iron lags 1-2 levels.
#   - Skip early statue/barracks/smith. No point boosting before mines are up.
#   - Bump warehouse ONLY when overflowing. Same for farm.
#   - Commit offensive after mines ~20/20/18 (axe + LC + rams, no defensive mix).
#   - Snob prerequisites: main 20, market 10, smith 20.
